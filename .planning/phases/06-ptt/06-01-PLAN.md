<objective>
Group text on unit channels, the base for push-to-talk (06-02 sends voice frames the same way).
</objective>

<design>
- Packet type `channel` (0x05), recipient = broadcast, fragmented like other messages, flooded.
- Payload: tag(8) || nonce(12) || ChaCha20-Poly1305(counter u64 || text) || mac(16).
  Associated data: label `offmesh/channel-msg/v1`, tag, sender PeerID.
- Counter: sender's clock in milliseconds, forced to increase. Receiver keeps a replay window
  of 60 s per (channel, sender), at most 256 senders remembered.
- Joined channel names are stored in the secret store (`channels.v1`, max 16) and deleted by
  panic wipe. Keys are re-derived whenever the unit changes.
</design>

<limits>
- One key per channel: any member of a channel can read all of it and could forge another
  member's sender ID. Per-sender signatures are not built.
- No delivery receipts on channels.
- A sender whose clock jumps back more than 60 s is ignored until its clock catches up.
</limits>
